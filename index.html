◆ About Me

My journey in technology started with a simple curiosity — how can software solve real problems for real people? That question led me to co-develop AcadTrack as part of a three-developer team, an AI-powered Academic Management System deployed across schools in the Visayas and showcased at the 2nd INNOVEX 2026 International Conference.

I approach every project by understanding the people it serves first, then building clean, reliable systems that deliver measurable value. My technical foundation spans HTML, CSS, Python, Flask, Java, JavaScript, Git & GitHub, and MariaDB — and I'm continuously expanding into Node.js, React Native, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and MySQL Workbench.

My goal is simple: keep learning, keep building, and keep creating technology that makes a meaningful difference.
